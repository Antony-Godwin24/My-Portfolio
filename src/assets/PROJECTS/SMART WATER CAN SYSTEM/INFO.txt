Title: Smart QR Water Can Monitoring System (AURA’24)

Problem:
Plastic water cans are widely reused without proper tracking, leading to contamination, bacterial growth, and serious health risks due to overuse and poor maintenance.

Solution:
Developed a QR-based system that tracks each water can’s usage, refilling cycles, and quality status through a centralized platform.

Tech:
Node.js, Express.js, MySQL, QR Code Integration

My Role:
Designed and implemented the backend system, QR-based tracking logic, and data management for monitoring water can lifecycle.

Key Features:
- Unique QR code assigned to each water can
- Tracks usage count and refilling history
- Prevents usage beyond safe lifecycle limits
- Ensures quality validation before reuse
- Enables transparency for both users and suppliers

System Design:
- Centralized database to store usage and refilling data
- QR-based identification for each water can
- Validation logic to restrict unsafe usage
- Real-time tracking and lifecycle monitoring

Impact:
Improves water safety, reduces health risks, and introduces a scalable system for monitoring reusable containers.

Achievement:
🥉 3rd Prize – AURA’24 Technical Hackathon (100+ teams)

Extension:
