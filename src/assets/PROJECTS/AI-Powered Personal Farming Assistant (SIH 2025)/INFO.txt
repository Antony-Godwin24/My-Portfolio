Title: AI-Powered Personal Farming Assistant (SIH 2025)

GitHub:
https://github.com/Antony-Godwin24/Al-Powered-Personal-Farming-Assistant-for-Kerala-Farmers

Problem:
Smallholder farmers in Kerala lack access to personalized, timely, and localized agricultural guidance. Existing systems are generic, English-based, and not accessible to farmers with low digital literacy.

Solution:
Developed an AI-powered farming assistant that provides personalized crop advice, weather alerts, and guidance using voice and text in the local language.

Tech:
React Native, Node.js (Express.js), MySQL, Rasa NLU, AI/NLP APIs

My Role:
Contributed to system design and backend development, including handling user interaction logic and integrating AI-based responses.

Key Features:
- Voice and text-based interaction for ease of use
- Malayalam language support for accessibility
- Personalized crop advisory based on farm data
- Real-time weather and disaster alerts (floods, rainfall)
- Activity tracking for farming operations

System Design:
- Mobile app interface connected to backend APIs
- AI/NLP engine (Rasa) for understanding user queries
- Database for storing farm data and activity logs
- Real-time alert system based on location and conditions

Impact:
Improves accessibility to modern farming knowledge, helps farmers make better decisions, and reduces risks from climate and environmental changes.

Context:
Developed as part of Smart India Hackathon (SIH 2025) under Agriculture & Rural Development theme.

Insight:
Focused on inclusivity by designing a system that works for users with low digital literacy through voice-first interaction.
